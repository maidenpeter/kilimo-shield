Method

  • MODIS MOD13Q1 NDVI, 250 m, 16-day composites, good or marginal pixels only, averaged over a 10 by 10 km box.
  • Seasonal index: the mean NDVI over each rainy season (long rains March to May, short rains October to December), expressed as a z-score against 2001 to 2025.
  • Trigger: the 20th percentile, roughly one bad season in five. Each season is judged against a threshold built from the other years only (leave one out), so the result is not forced to 20%.
  • Real schemes in Kenya (IBLI, KLIP) use their own NDVI products and area units; this test does not reproduce them.

Findings

  • Around Laisamis, Marsabit County, long rains, March to May: 25 seasons of data; a one-in-five line would have been crossed in 6 of them (2006, 2009, 2011, 2012, 2017, 2022).
  • Around Laisamis, Marsabit County, short rains, October to December: 25 seasons of data; a one-in-five line would have been crossed in 5 of them (2005, 2009, 2010, 2021, 2022).
  • Around Merti, Isiolo County, long rains, March to May: 25 seasons of data; a one-in-five line would have been crossed in 6 of them (2001, 2009, 2011, 2012, 2015, 2017).
  • Around Merti, Isiolo County, short rains, October to December: 25 seasons of data; a one-in-five line would have been crossed in 6 of them (2005, 2007, 2008, 2009, 2010, 2012).
  • Two sites about 110 km apart, long rains, March to May: the seasonal scores move together (correlation 0.787), but of the 8 seasons below the line at either site, only 4 were below it at both. Which seasons trigger depends on where the index is measured, so one index for a wide area would carry real basis risk: units need to be small.
  • Two sites about 110 km apart, short rains, October to December: the seasonal scores move together (correlation 0.852), but of the 8 seasons below the line at either site, only 3 were below it at both. Which seasons trigger depends on where the index is measured, so one index for a wide area would carry real basis risk: units need to be small.
  • Around Laisamis, Marsabit County: short rains 2016 z -0.49, long rains 2017 z -0.80; below the line in 1 of the 2 seasons in which KLIP paid out (in other counties).
  • Around Merti, Isiolo County: short rains 2016 z -0.74, long rains 2017 z -1.03; below the line in 1 of the 2 seasons in which KLIP paid out (in other counties).

Do the two sites agree?

  • Long rains, March to May: both sites below the line in 2009, 2011, 2012, 2017; correlation of the two sites' scores 0.787.
  • Short rains, October to December: both sites below the line in 2005, 2009, 2010; correlation of the two sites' scores 0.852.

What would still be needed

  • Real insurance units (for example sub-locations) and their boundaries instead of 10 km boxes.
  • Livestock loss or body-condition data to measure basis risk; without it, the trigger can only be checked against other indices.
  • A forage-season definition agreed with pastoral experts for each area, and a check against known drought years.
  • A licensed partner insurer (to be named) and regulator approval before anything is offered.
