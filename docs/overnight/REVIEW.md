# Overnight review

Five independent reviewers read the code (security and data protection; money, dates and sales windows; accessibility
and performance; tests, docs and deployment; public-site honesty). A second, skeptical reviewer then tried to refute
every finding against the code; only findings that survived are listed as real. Raw results: `review_findings.json`.

54 findings: 50 confirmed, 4 refuted.
Fixed tonight: 27. Needs approval: 17. Later (low risk, not urgent): 6.

Rule followed: anything touching pricing, payouts, money handling, consent, the auth and role design, masking rules or
data retention is listed under 'needs approval' and left unchanged, except clear bugs fixed with a test (noted).

## Refuted by the second reviewer

- **Plain officers can bulk-enrol and export the whole SACCO policy register, although these are documented as manager-only** (`app.py:1182`): Refuted as a privilege problem. Officer access to /bulk is deliberate: test_m6b.py:110 fetches /bulk as an officer and asserts only that the KIAMIS section stays manager-only. test_platform.py:364 exports as the default officer. The export is limited to off.sacco and masks national ID and phone (services.py:519-525). Its contents (name, member_no, status, balance, loan_ref) are the same own-SACCO 
- **M-Pesa B2C payout sends a half-even rounded amount, but the payout is recorded as paid in full** (`monitoring.py:235`): Not reachable as described. DarajaClient.b2c (mpesa.py:85-88) always raises MpesaError ('not configured yet'), so production sends no B2C money. Only SimulatedDaraja.b2c succeeds, and it moves no money. No M-Pesa statement exists to fall out of reconciliation, and no farmer is short-paid. At most this is a latent concern for when B2C is built, not a confirmed bug.
- **No migrations and no PostgreSQL driver, although the docs direct deployment to PostgreSQL** (`db.py:25`): The facts are right (create_all only, no Alembic, no psycopg), but the repo documents this as a known pre-production state, not a defect. README.md:229-230 says 'Migrations (Alembic) come before any real data.' .env.example:8-9 puts the postgresql DATABASE_URL under 'Later milestones (placeholders, not used yet)', and README:197 says PostgreSQL 'come[s] for deployment'. - **Public footer labels the insurer and SACCO links "Partners", implying partnerships that do not exist** (`base.html:120`): Weak. 'Partners' in base.html:120 is a navigation heading for audiences, with links named 'For insurers', 'For SACCOs' and 'Request a demo'. It does not name any partner. The fine print directly below in the same footer says 'Cover is underwritten by a partner insurer (to be named)', and insurers.html:96 says no insurer has approved the product. A reasonable visitor would not read this as a claim 

The footer heading 'Partners' was renamed 'Work with us' anyway, to avoid any hint of a partnership.
