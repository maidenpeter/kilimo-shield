Your maize cover: what you need to know

What it is. Insurance for your maize in the long rains. It pays when the rain in your ward is too little for maize. The rain is measured by satellite for the whole ward. Nobody visits your farm to check, and you do not make a claim.

Who is who. The insurer is the {{ insurer }}. Your SACCO sells the cover and collects your payments. AgroShield runs the system that measures the rain and works out payouts.

What it pays.

  • If the season's rain is too poor for maize in your ward (the ward score falls below {{ lb.trigger_wrsi }} out of 100), you get part of your cover. The worse the season, the more you get, up to all of your cover when the score is {{ lb.exit_wrsi }} or less.
  • If the planting rains never come (less than {{ fo.window_rain_below_mm|int }} mm in the whole planting window), you get {{ fo.payout|pct }} of your cover.
  • If it stays dry for {{ gd.days_after_planting }} days after the planting rain (less than {{ gd.rain_below_mm|int }} mm), you get a seed voucher worth KES {{ "{:,.0f}".format(gd.replant_cost_kes_per_acre) }} per acre to plant again.

What it does not do. It follows the rain in your ward, not on your own farm. Your crop can fail when the ward score shows no payout, or the other way round. If this happens, tell your SACCO officer or use option 5 "Report crop loss" on the USSD menu. Your report is recorded and checked, but it does not change the payout. The cover only measures lack of rain. It does not pay for pests, disease, floods or theft.

When your cover starts. Only when your share of the price is fully paid by the sales closing date. You can pay in parts, by M-Pesa or by SACCO deduction. If it is not fully paid by that date there is no cover, and what you paid is given back through your SACCO.

The price. The government ({{ sub.programme }}) pays {{ sub.government_share|pct }} of the price for farms of {{ sub.eligible_acres.min }} to {{ sub.eligible_acres.max }} acres. You pay the rest. Good farming practice can lower your share (Resilience discount). Your SACCO officer tells you the exact amount before you agree.

When you are paid. Within {{ mon.payout_within_days }} days after the ward score is confirmed. Rain data arrives about three weeks after each month ends, so the score is confirmed after the season. You are paid by M-Pesa, or into your SACCO loan if you agreed to that.

Messages. You get SMS about your cover and payouts only if you agree.

Your information. Your national ID and phone number are stored locked (encrypted) and shown only partly. They are used only for this cover and shared with the insurer only for this cover.

Bima ya mahindi yako: unachohitaji kujua

Ni nini. Bima ya mahindi yako wakati wa masika. Inalipa ikiwa mvua katika wadi yako haitoshi kwa mahindi. Mvua inapimwa kwa satelaiti kwa wadi nzima. Hakuna anayekuja shambani kwako kukagua, na huhitaji kudai.

Nani ni nani. Kampuni ya bima ni {{ insurer }} (kampuni ya bima mshirika, itatajwa). SACCO yako inauza bima hii na kupokea malipo yako. AgroShield inaendesha mfumo unaopima mvua na kuhesabu malipo.

Inalipa nini.

  • Ikiwa mvua ya msimu ni duni kwa mahindi katika wadi yako (alama ya wadi iko chini ya {{ lb.trigger_wrsi }} kati ya 100), unapata sehemu ya kiasi cha bima yako. Msimu ukiwa mbaya zaidi, unapata zaidi, hadi kiasi chote alama ikiwa {{ lb.exit_wrsi }} au chini.
  • Ikiwa mvua ya kupanda haitanyesha kabisa (chini ya mm {{ fo.window_rain_below_mm|int }} katika kipindi chote cha kupanda), unapata asilimia {{ (fo.payout * 100)|round|int }} ya kiasi cha bima yako.
  • Ikiwa kutakuwa na ukavu kwa siku {{ gd.days_after_planting }} baada ya mvua ya kupanda (chini ya mm {{ gd.rain_below_mm|int }}), unapata vocha ya mbegu ya KES {{ "{:,.0f}".format(gd.replant_cost_kes_per_acre) }} kwa ekari ili upande tena.

Isiyolipa. Bima inafuata mvua ya wadi yako, si ya shamba lako peke yake. Mazao yako yanaweza kuharibika wakati alama ya wadi haionyeshi malipo, au kinyume chake. Hili likitokea, mwambie afisa wa SACCO yako au tumia chaguo 5 "Ripoti hasara" kwenye menyu ya USSD. Ripoti yako inarekodiwa na kukaguliwa, lakini haibadilishi malipo. Bima hii inapima ukosefu wa mvua pekee. Hailipii wadudu, magonjwa, mafuriko wala wizi.

Bima inaanza lini. Pale tu sehemu yako ya bei imelipwa yote kufikia tarehe ya mwisho ya mauzo. Unaweza kulipa kwa awamu, kwa M-Pesa au kwa makato ya SACCO. Isipolipwa yote kufikia tarehe hiyo hakuna bima, na pesa uliyolipa inarudishwa kupitia SACCO yako.

Bei. Serikali ({{ sub.programme }}) inalipa asilimia {{ (sub.government_share * 100)|round|int }} ya bei kwa mashamba ya ekari {{ sub.eligible_acres.min }} hadi {{ sub.eligible_acres.max }}. Wewe unalipa iliyobaki. Kilimo bora kinaweza kupunguza sehemu yako (punguzo la Resilience). Afisa wa SACCO yako atakuambia kiasi kamili kabla hujakubali.

Utalipwa lini. Ndani ya siku {{ mon.payout_within_days }} baada ya alama ya wadi kuthibitishwa. Takwimu za mvua hufika takriban wiki tatu baada ya kila mwezi kuisha, kwa hiyo alama inathibitishwa baada ya msimu. Unalipwa kwa M-Pesa, au kwenye mkopo wako wa SACCO ikiwa ulikubali hivyo.

Ujumbe. Unapata SMS kuhusu bima yako na malipo ikiwa tu umekubali.

Taarifa zako. Nambari yako ya kitambulisho na ya simu zimehifadhiwa kwa usimbaji na zinaonyeshwa sehemu tu. Zinatumika kwa bima hii pekee na zinashirikiwa na kampuni ya bima kwa bima hii pekee.
