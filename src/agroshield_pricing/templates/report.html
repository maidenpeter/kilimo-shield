Method

Dekads rain and PET summed over days 1-10, 11-20, 21-end of each month Failed onset window rain < threshold: fixed share of sum insured, no WRSI season Start of season first dekad in the window with rain >= planting_rain_mm (window end if none) Germination rain in the N days after planting < threshold: replanting cost paid, start moves to next planting rain Each crop dekad Kc from the FEWS NET maize curve at the dekad's share of the growing period WR = Kc x PET Ks = min(1, (soil + rain) / ((1 - p) x capacity)) AET = min(Ks x WR, soil + rain); soil = min(capacity, soil + rain - AET) WRSI 100 x sum(AET) / sum(WR) at the end of the growing period Season payout min(SI, failed-onset share x SI or clip((trigger - WRSI) / (trigger - exit), 0, 1) x SI + replanting KES) Gross premium (expected loss + risk margin) / (1 - sum of loadings), per sum-insured option in KES

WRSI weights every dekad's shortfall by that dekad's water requirement, whatever the growth stage, so a deficit concentrated at flowering in an otherwise wet season is diluted. CHIRPS daily values are disaggregated from five-day totals; the index only uses dekadal sums. PET is Hargreaves from NASA POWER temperature, shifted to the ward's elevation, because FEWS NET Penman-Monteith PET was unreachable.
