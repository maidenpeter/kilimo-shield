What your officers do

Built for a busy office and a basic phone.

Enrol a member

A few short screens: the member, the farm, consent, then the cover and payment. A price answer in plain words before anything is saved.

Collect premium

By SACCO deduction or M-Pesa, in instalments. Cover starts only when the member's share is fully paid by the sales close.

Follow payouts

Each member's cover and payouts in plain words, with the technical detail one tap away.

Report a missed loss

If a member's crop failed and the index did not pay, record it. Every report is reviewed.

What managers see

  • Members, policies and payments for your SACCO only.
  • Premium collected and what is still owed to the insurer.
  • Ward rainfall status and any payouts on the way.
  • Bulk import and KIAMIS checks, reports and approved weather advisories.

Member ID numbers and phone numbers are encrypted when stored and masked on every screen.

What members get

A payout when the ward's rains fail, a seed voucher if the rain stops after planting, and the option to send a payout to their SACCO loan first. They can check their cover by USSD on any phone, in English or Kiswahili.

The government programme describes a 50% premium subsidy for farms of 0.5 to 20 acres{{ ui.cite(1) }}. Whether this cover qualifies is not confirmed yet; AgroShield shows that share as an assumption, "not yet received", until it arrives.

What it costs the SACCO

The price includes a SACCO distribution fee, to be set with the insurer. The illustrative pricing uses 5% of the gross premium as a placeholder.
