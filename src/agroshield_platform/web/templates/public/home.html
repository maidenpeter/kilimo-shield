Reproducible pricing

Each ward is priced from CHIRPS satellite rainfall, 1981 to 2025{{ ui.cite(1) }}. Every pricing run saves its inputs, config hashes and a results checksum, so any premium can be rebuilt.

How pricing works

Ward-level triggers

Payouts follow a crop water index (WRSI), a method first developed by FAO and used by FEWS NET{{ ui.cite(2) }}, worked out for each ward, never for a whole county.

About the crop index
{{ ui.icon('pulse') }}

Payout monitoring

Every ward is checked daily. When a trigger is confirmed, the payout waits for your approval or hold, with a clock that counts 10 days from that confirmation. Kenya's draft index insurance regulations, as reported, count ten days from the trigger{{ ui.cite(3) }}, so the two can differ.

See triggers and payouts
{{ ui.icon('table') }}

Bordereaux and audit

Monthly premium and claims bordereaux with a manifest and checksum, a reinsurer summary, and a log of every policy, payment and payout event.

About the audit trail

Who the cover protects

Farmers first, then herders

When the rains fail in a ward, every insured farmer there is paid the same way, with no farm visit and no claim form.

  • A drought payout when the season's crop water index falls below the payout line.
  • A replanting seed voucher if the rain stops soon after planting.
  • Cover checked on any phone by USSD, in English or Kiswahili.
